# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Nexa CRM is intended for B2B sales and account-management teams, including digital agencies, software houses, consulting and service companies, real-estate sales teams, small SaaS teams, and founder-led companies. Primary roles are sales representatives, sales managers, account managers, and workspace administrators.

## Product Purpose

Give teams one structured place to understand customer relationships and decide what should happen next. Success means teams can capture and qualify leads, convert them into contacts, companies, and deals, move deals through a pipeline, record interactions, and follow up with tasks.

## Positioning

The MVP focuses on sales execution and relationship management rather than a complete ERP or general project-management tool.

## Operating Context

Teams currently spread sales information across spreadsheets, chat, email, personal notes, and calendars. Representatives use the CRM to track prospects, ownership, activity, and follow-ups; managers use it to understand pipeline health, forecasts, and deals at risk; account managers use it to retain customer history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The MVP covers workspaces and role-based access, leads, contacts, companies, deals and pipelines, tasks, activities and timelines, notes, search, reports, notifications, and import/export as defined by the product brief and PRD. Workspaces must remain isolated, permissions must be enforced server-side and by database policies, and dashboard/report values must come from real CRM data. This product is not intended to become a complete ERP. The name “Nexa CRM” is a working placeholder and is not locked.

## Product Principles

- Keep relationship history and ownership understandable in one workspace.
- Make the next follow-up action easy to identify.
- Give teams a clear, configurable sales pipeline without expanding into ERP scope.
- Show operational and reporting data derived from actual records.
- Preserve workspace isolation and role-based access throughout the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support visible keyboard focus, keyboard navigation, properly labeled forms and controls, semantic buttons and links, sufficient contrast, and layouts that remain usable on mobile. Dialogs must be keyboard usable and maintain logical focus behavior.
