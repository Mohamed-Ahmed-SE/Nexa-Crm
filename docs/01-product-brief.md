# 01 — Product Brief

## Working product name

**Nexa CRM**

The name is only a placeholder and can be replaced without changing product behavior.

## Product category

B2B Sales CRM and relationship management workspace.

## Primary target customers

- digital agencies,
- software houses,
- consulting teams,
- real-estate sales teams,
- B2B service companies,
- small SaaS sales teams,
- account-management teams,
- founder-led companies that currently manage sales in spreadsheets or WhatsApp.

## Primary user personas

### 1. Sales Representative

Needs to know:

- which leads to contact,
- which deals are moving,
- what task is due,
- what happened with a prospect,
- what action should happen next.

### 2. Sales Manager

Needs to know:

- pipeline health,
- deal ownership,
- overdue follow-ups,
- stage conversion,
- forecast value,
- sales rep activity,
- deals at risk.

### 3. Account Manager

Needs to see:

- customer history,
- contacts,
- notes,
- tasks,
- meetings,
- current and previous deals,
- relationship activity.

### 4. Workspace Admin

Needs to manage:

- users,
- roles,
- pipeline stages,
- custom tags,
- workspace settings,
- imports,
- access.

## Core customer problem

Sales information is often fragmented across spreadsheets, messages, email inboxes, notes, calendars, and individual memory.

This causes:

- forgotten follow-ups,
- duplicated work,
- weak visibility,
- inconsistent sales process,
- missing customer history,
- unclear deal ownership,
- inaccurate forecasts.

## Product promise

Nexa CRM gives the team one structured place to understand every relationship and know what should happen next.

## MVP positioning

The MVP is intentionally focused on sales execution rather than becoming a complete ERP.

It should be:

- faster to understand than Salesforce,
- more structured than spreadsheets,
- more focused than a general project-management tool,
- flexible enough for different B2B teams.

## MVP success outcomes

A workspace should be able to:

1. create or import leads,
2. qualify them,
3. convert them into contacts/companies/deals,
4. move deals through the pipeline,
5. create and complete follow-up tasks,
6. record interactions,
7. see a complete relationship timeline,
8. understand sales performance from the dashboard and reports,
9. collaborate using shared data,
10. manage access safely.

## Explicitly outside MVP

Do not build these unless the core MVP is finished:

- full email inbox synchronization,
- telephony integration,
- WhatsApp integration,
- marketing automation,
- advanced workflow automation builder,
- AI sales assistant,
- invoicing/accounting,
- customer support ticketing,
- custom object builder,
- public API marketplace,
- complex territory management,
- multi-currency accounting,
- CPQ,
- commissions engine.

The architecture should leave room for these later.
